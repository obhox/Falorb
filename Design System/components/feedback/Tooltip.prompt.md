One-line: glass hover surface — terse label form for controls, `rows` form for chart readouts.

```jsx
<Tooltip label="June 2026" rows={[{label:"Visitors",value:"33,800"},{label:"Sessions",value:"41,209",color:"var(--series-2)"}]}>
  <span>hover target</span>
</Tooltip>
```

Fades only (160ms) — no slide, no scale.
